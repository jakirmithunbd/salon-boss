About Us 🚀

Pioneering the Digital Space for Hair and Beauty Industry Since 2017

Our Story

Welcome to Salon Boss, the go-to digital marketing agency exclusively for the hair and beauty industry. Our journey began in 2017, with a vision of helping individual stylists transform their online presence. Founded by Matt Peters, a seasoned digital marketing expert with a strong foundation in the beauty sector, Salon Boss swiftly became a trusted ally for professionals struggling to navigate the technical and business side of the industry.

Evolution of Salon Boss

Over the years, Salon Boss evolved as Matt saw a wider need in the industry. After two years of careful re-evaluation and strategic planning, Salon Boss was rebooted in 2022 with an expanded mission - to empower not just individual stylists, but salons, salon suites, and hair/beauty brands. We now stand as a digital marketing leader, committed to helping businesses with a growth mindset scale new heights in the digital landscape.

Meet our Founder

Matt's journey began in 2013 when he started consulting for a multi-million dollar U.S. hair extension company. The experience opened his eyes to the unique marketing challenges faced by beauty professionals. With a decade of advanced marketing strategies under his belt, Matt now brings his expertise to small to medium hair & beauty businesses, helping them elevate their digital footprint and achieve their next level of growth.

Our Mission & Values

At Salon Boss, our mission is to empower hair and beauty businesses to thrive in the digital age. We bring together advanced marketing strategies, intimate industry knowledge, and a passion for helping others succeed. Our values are built on innovation, growth, expertise, and integrity, which are reflected in every project we undertake.

Trust in Us
