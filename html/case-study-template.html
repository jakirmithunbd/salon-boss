Transforming an Invisible Business into a Local Hair Styling Powerhouse

To achieve Aleah's goals, Salon Boss implemented a comprehensive SEO and web design strategy. We began by performing a thorough competitive analysis and keyword research to understand the local market and identify opportunities for Sapphire Hair to stand out. With this foundation, we designed a custom website for Sapphire Hair that not only matched Aleah's branding but also highlighted her expertise in hair extensions. This new website was fully optimized for both SEO and conversions, giving us complete control over on-page SEO factors and providing a professional platform to showcase Aleah's work.

Next, we established and optimized Sapphire Hair's Google Business Profile. This crucial step improved local visibility and provided potential clients with essential information about the business. To further enhance Sapphire Hair's online reputation, we set Aleah up with our reputation management software, empowering her to generate and manage client reviews effectively.

To strengthen Sapphire Hair's online presence, we built out online citations across relevant platforms. This not only improved local SEO but also ensured consistent business information across the web. Additionally, we created engaging, SEO-driven blog articles to showcase Aleah's expertise and attract potential clients searching for hair styling information online.

Throughout the process, we continuously analyzed our efforts, made necessary adjustments, and focused on sustainable growth. Our SEO package was carefully crafted to take Sapphire Hair from zero visibility to ranking in the top 3, proving especially effective for solo professionals like Aleah.
